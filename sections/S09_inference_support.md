# S09 - Inference support

`results/final/bootstrap_validity_summary.csv` records interval validity by domain,
horizon, and metric. `supplement_tables/S09_bootstrap_target_support_summary.csv` records
the independent-unit boundary. `results/final/target_cluster_registry.csv` supplies the
recording roster, including the inD four-location counts 11, 12, 1, and 7.

The exact sign-change lower bound is 2^(1-G): 0.500 for G=2 and 0.125 for G=4. The
recording-bootstrap composition support is three at G=2 and at most thirty-five at G=4.
