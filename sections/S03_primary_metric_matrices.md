# S03 - Primary metric matrices

`results/final/primary_model_comparator_contrasts.csv` contains the complete 18-cell by
four-family contrast matrices for Average Precision and Recall@5% against all three
comparator families. `supplement_tables/S03_learned_model_metrics.csv`,
`supplement_tables/S03_roc_auc_results.csv`, and
`results/final/four_model_primary_metrics.csv` provide the learned-family metric views.
