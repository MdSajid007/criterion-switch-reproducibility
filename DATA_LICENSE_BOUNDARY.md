# Data and licence boundary

No ETH, UCY, or inD trajectory corpus is included. Obtain each corpus from its official distribution point and follow its terms.

The inD distribution terms prohibit redistribution of the corpus or modified versions. Whether prepared row-level decision-point, pair-observation, episode, and feature tables qualify for redistribution has not been resolved. They are therefore withheld. The same conservative boundary is used for all three corpora in this repository.

Distributed CSV files contain aggregate metrics or contrast-level result rows. They contain no positions, velocities, per-agent trajectories, or per-frame observations. Contrast-level result rows must not be confused with source-data rows.

No public software or documentation licence has been applied to this repository. Contact the rights holders for reuse permissions. Third-party corpus terms remain independent.
